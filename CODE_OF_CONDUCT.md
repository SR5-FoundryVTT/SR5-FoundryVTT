# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive
feedback, and focus disagreements on ideas and observable behavior. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose
behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/SR5-FoundryVTT/SR5-FoundryVTT/security/advisories/new). Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
